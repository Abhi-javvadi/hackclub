PHOENIX RUN

You are a phoenix. Hold ↑ / W to rise, ↓ / S to dive.

Collect embers to grow. Bigger means more points per ember, but a bigger target.

Crashing never ends the run. You burn, and seconds later you are reborn smaller and faster. Every rebirth makes the fire hotter.

Press SPACE to fly
